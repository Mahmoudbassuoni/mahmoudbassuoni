# 👋 Hello, World! I'm Mahmoud Bassyouni

🔬 **Senior Bioinformatician | PhD Researcher | Genomics & AI**

I develop reproducible bioinformatics workflows and integrate genomic, functional annotation and expression data to support biological interpretation.

🌍 **About Me**
- 🧬 Bachelor’s in Biomedical Sciences from Zewail City of Science and Technology
- 📊 Master’s in Informatics from Nile University
- 🎓 Both degrees recognised by the University of Porto, Portugal
- 🌿 Currently pursuing a PhD at the University of Porto, with research based at CIIMAR

⚡ **What I Do**
- **Genomic analysis:** WGS/WES workflows, variant calling, pharmacogenomic interpretation and reporting
- **Functional annotation:** Integration of eggNOG, InterPro, Gene Ontology and KEGG annotations
- **Database development:** Relational data modelling with SQL and SQLite, identifier harmonisation and annotation-source traceability
- **Workflow development:** Reproducible pipelines using Nextflow, nf-core and Docker
- **Validation:** Variant-calling benchmarking against gold-standard truth sets using precision, recall and F1 scores
- **Genomics AI:** Machine learning for comparative functional genomics and seaweed genotype–phenotype studies

🔭 **Current Research**

My current work includes developing a SQLite resource linking approximately **1.42 million proteins across 50 taxa** with functional annotation and expression data for comparative analysis and downstream explainable AI.

📈 **Selected Experience**
- Developed a pharmacogenomics proficiency-testing workflow integrating **nf-core/sarek, StellarPGx and PharmCAT**, from FASTQ preprocessing to star-allele interpretation and reporting
- Built genomic analysis workflows and configured remote Linux servers for bioinformatics analysis
- Implemented automated sequencing-data archiving and secure data transfers
- Investigated predicted variant effects on protein structure using **Missense3D and AlphaFold**
- Conducted positive-selection analyses of mitochondrial coding sequences for the **Vertebrate Genome Project**
- Published research in [Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-46844-z) and the [Journal of Biological Inorganic Chemistry](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00775-017-1530-8)

🛠️ **Tools & Technologies**

- **Programming:** Python · R · Bash · SQL
- **Workflows:** Nextflow · nf-core · Docker · Git/GitHub
- **Data & Infrastructure:** SQLite · Linux · SSH · AWS familiarity
- **Genomics:** BWA · SAMtools · GATK · PLINK · PharmCAT · StellarPGx
- **Functional Annotation:** eggNOG · InterPro · GO · KEGG · Pfam
- **Machine Learning:** scikit-learn · PyTorch · TensorFlow

💡 **What Motivates Me**

I enjoy turning complex biological datasets into interpretable results.
